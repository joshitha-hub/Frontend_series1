04 COMMUNITY FAQ
Do I need to be an expert to join?+

No. Curiosity is enough. The goal is to learn by doing and grow alongside other builders.

What can I build here?+

Anything from small interactive pages and creative experiments to larger team projects and product prototypes.

Is the cluster only about frontend?+

The Web Cluster focuses on the web, but the work naturally touches design, JavaScript, product thinking and collaboration.
